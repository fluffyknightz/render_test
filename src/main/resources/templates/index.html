Introduction

Our Visions

A society rooted in Christian values of Charity, Justice, and Peace; where Integral Human Development reaches everyone.

Our Missions

To establish the enabling environment of fraternal cooperation and co-management among the commissions, offices, and institutions of the Myanmar Catholic Church to realize their mission.

  • Enabling environment = System Strengthening

  • Fraternal Cooperation = Church mission-oriented

  • Co-Management = Pooling and sharing the resources for joint mission

Our Objectives

  • To facilitate an efficient and sustained coordination mechanism among the commissions, offices, and institutions in implementing the socio-pastoral strategic plan.

  • To coordinate key stakeholders’ advocacy and networking at the CBCM level in ensuring fundraising, effective resource allocations, and relationship empowerment.

  • To animate the institutional development and human resource development mechanism for the Myanmar Catholic Church.

  • To advocate and coordinate the Data and Information Management System (DIMS) at the CBCM level.

Our Values

Christian Charity

  • Grounded in the Gospel, we embody Christ’s selfless love and extend acts of charity to all, recognizing the inherent dignity of every individual.

Love and Care

  • Practicing love and care, we address the diverse needs of individuals within coordinated socio-pastoral actions, ensuring a holistic approach to their well-being.

Harmony And Unity

  • Inspired by the Church’s teachings on unity, we actively foster harmonious collaboration within socio-pastoral initiatives, emphasizing the strength derived from coordinated efforts.

Integral Human Development

Fraternal Cooperation for Mission

  • Embracing the Church’s mission, our core value centers on fraternal cooperation for the mission, where collaborative efforts are strategically aligned to efficiently fulfill the socio-pastoral mission entrusted to the Myanmar Catholic Church.

Our Strategic Direction

What is Our Strategic Direction?

On August 17, 290016, the Holy Father Pope Francis established the Dicastery for Promoting Integral Human Development. The apostolic letter stated that “the Church is called to promote the integral development of the human person in the light of the Gospel”, and the Catholic Bishops’ Conference of Myanmar (CBCM) incorporated this papal guidance into its “Socio-Pastoral Strategic Plan a contribution to Nation Building (2018-2022). Rooted in articulating the Vatican II document Gaudium et Spes and encyclical Caritas in Veritate, the integral care of the human being is a vocation of every human person. CBCM consistently embraced this mission to the “Myanmar Catholic Church Synodal Journey, Socio-Pastoral Strategic Plan (2024-2026)” document holistically and synergistically addressing the Myanmar people suffering through the 6 external strategic directions.

External

Education

To ensure that safe and high-quality education remains accessible during the ongoing crisis in Myanmar and to support children not currently attending school to enter or return to quality learning opportunities.

Health

Enhance primary healthcare access by delivering essential routine basic healthcare services, identifying and managing emergency cases, and playing a crucial role in disease surveillance. Through our comprehensive health services, we aim to witness the loving care and healing ministry of Jesus Christ.

Migration

Ensure that migrants and their families receive protection, assistance, and empowerment to address their vulnerabilities and achieve sustainable reintegration.

Humanitarian

To ensure the well-being of those affected by the conflict in Myanmar while also maintaining their human dignity. We aim to achieve durable solutions that meet international standards whenever possible and to enhance respect for the rights of those who have been affected.

Safeguarding

The Bishops Conference of Myanmar develops and implements safeguarding standards to ensure that every Church entity, commission, and congregation across Myanmar places the safety of children and adults at the forefront of their planning, thinking, and actions.

Faith Formation

The Church will assist individual of all ages in developing and achieving their Christian maturity as human beings.

Inter-Religious Engagement and Ecumenical Engagement

Interreligious and ecumenical dialogue is a simple concept: persons of different faiths meet to have a conversation. It is a conversation among people of different faiths on a common subject, the primary purpose of which is for each participant to learn from the other to change and grow. It is not a debate. Through interreligious and ecumenical engagement, the ‘Elite Leadership’ of high-level religious leaders for collective advocacy and joint action for peace is strengthened. Interfaith dialogue can strengthen the ‘Elite Interfaith body’ for mediation between conflicting parties. Interfaith dialogue can bring people from different religions to nurture agents of reconciliation.

Environmental Justice

Laudate Deum: While the previous Nation Building strategic plan referred to Laudato Si encyclical, This Synodal Journey strategic plan followed the apostolic exhortation Laudate Deum in which the Holy Father pointed out the imminent global climate crisis, unchecked technocratic growth undermining the human responsibilities, weaken international politics, the importance of climate conference COP and end up His document with spiritual motivation. He urges for more durable actions so that our common home is saved. Following this, the Synodal journey strategic plan emphasized Environmental justice as one of the 4 internal strategic directions.
